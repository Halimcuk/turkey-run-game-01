Gobble Dash is a side-scrolling turkey escape runner: Gobbles the turkey bolts out of the farmyard with the farmer and his carving knife a few steps behind, jumping fences and glowing ovens, sliding under hay bales, dodging swinging and flying knives, and grabbing corn kernels and pumpkin-pie slices across the barnyard, kitchen garden and pine forest on a 1000-metre dash to the forest gate, with three lives, rising speed and difficulty, synthesised gobbles and jingles, and a title, pause, caught and escaped screen all drawn by the game's own code.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```
